## Fix 1: one id per SPEAKER (`clip.mjs buildLedgerLines`, `speakers.mjs speakerIdIssues`)
- A ledger line has `id` (`L1`, `L2`, ... the line, unique, the key of the audit and of `breakdown.lineIds`) and `sid` (`S1`, `S2`, ... the speaker): the first speaker to speak is S1, the next new speaker S2, and a speaker keeps its `sid` for the whole film. Speakers are the resolved cast or voice ids; an unresolved speaker string is its own speaker.
- The writer's facts (`writer.mjs e4Facts`) show `(Sx)`; `hybrid46/finish.mjs` and `finish2.mjs` write `L.sid` (the audit's `precedingSpeaker` only checks that an `(Sn)` exists; it never compared the number with the line id).
- Code check `speakerIdIssues({lines, written})`: in the ledger a speaker has exactly one id, no id is shared by two speakers, and ids are numbered by first speaking; in every written clip (rows of `speakerAttribution`) the id in the prompt is the speaker's id. Run over the whole film in `filmchecks.mjs` (`film_checks.json`, `speakerIds`).

## Fix 2: an unseen voice is not a `<Subject N>` (`clip.mjs`, `writer.mjs`, `speakers.mjs`, `hybrid46`)
- `assembleReferences` no longer puts a voice in `references`: no subject paragraph, no subject number, no retention line, no picture. `makeFix` keeps `voices` (voice phrase, where it carries from) for the writer.
- A line whose speaker is a voice (`lineOffscreen`: `voice: true, offscreen: true`) is written "a stable voice description (Sx), off-screen, says: `<d>`..." in one sentence with no `<Subject N>` (ref guide 5.4); a visible character who speaks from out of frame (`lineOffscreen`: not named in the shot's subject and the shot text puts the source out of frame) keeps `<Subject N> (Sx)` and is marked `off-screen`. Facts say so (`e4Facts`), `ensureLines` re-inserts a dropped voice line in that form, and `retagSpeakers` repairs the id, the marker and a stray `<Subject N>`.
- Speaker attribution (`speakerAttribution`, `attributionFindings`, `enforceAttribution`) is E4.1's check adapted: for a voice line the sentence must hold the line's `(Sx)` and the word off-screen and no `<Subject N>`; for a pictured speaker the nearest `<Subject N>` is the plan's speaker. Finding code `DIALOGUE_VOICE_FORM`. The audit's subject-based codes `DIALOGUE_NO_SPEAKER`, `DIALOGUE_SPEAKER_MISMATCH` and `DIALOGUE_SUBJECT_OUT_OF_RANGE` are dropped for a voice line in `hybrid46/validate.mjs strictValidation`, because the audit attributes through `<Subject N>`.
- **The off-screen exception** (copied lint modules, not frozen hybrid3): `hybrid46/negation.mjs` (the `guide_offscreen_marker` exemption, `OFFSCREEN_MARKER_CONTEXT`) and `hybrid46/lint3.mjs`. The word `off-screen` is the guide's own marker and is exempt only when its sentence carries an `(Sx)` speaker id or says "off-screen voice" / "off-screen voiceover"; anywhere else ("the lamp is off-screen") it is still a negated presence. Both files are `hybrid3/negation.mjs` and `hybrid3/lint3.mjs` plus that exception; a test counts the changed lines.
- `voiceFieldIssues` also rejects a music-like word in a voice description (fix 5), so the bible is asked again.

## Fix 3: framing of off-screen lines (`offscreen.mjs`)
- Decision (code, from the plan, no model): `offscreenCuts(clip, lines, bible)`: for each cut with an off-screen line, the cast members named in that planned shot's subject are on screen (mode `lips_shut`); when none is named the cut can show only the place (mode `no_face`, preferred when the plan allows). The decision is stored in `decisions.json` (`offscreen`) and on the shot.
- Writer: `e4Facts` states the mode per cut: no_face = "frame this cut on <the planned subject>: the place itself, with no character's face or body in view"; lips_shut = "write that the lips are pressed shut" in positive words.
- Check: `offscreenFramingRows` reads the written cut (`[Shot n]` segment): every character `<Subject N>` in it (the speaker excluded) must have a sentence stating lips/mouth pressed, closed, shut or sealed; a cut with no character passes. Finding `offscreen_framing` (sentence index of the first sentence about the character) goes through the existing targeted repair (`hybrid46/repair.mjs`, rule text in `hybrid46/validate.mjs`).

## Fix 4: exact utterances and sequence continuity (`utterance.mjs`, `sequence.mjs`, `planner.mjs`)
- **Planner prompt change (the only one): `UTTERANCE_RULE`**, appended after `CONTINUITY_RULE` and before the output shape. `vendor/prompts/planner.md` is untouched. The text:

  > UTTERANCE RULE — everything a character or a voice says aloud in the story, and everything the story implies is said aloud (counting, reciting, chanting, calling out, repeating another speaker, reading aloud), is written as the EXACT WORDS in that shot's dialogue_line, with has_dialogue true and the speaker in dialogue_speaker. Never describe speech in subject or action ("she counts aloud", "her mouth moves in a steady count", "the voice calls back"): write the words themselves ("One. Two. Three."). A sequence (numbers, a list, a chant) is written out in full, by the speaker the story names, in the order the story gives. When the story relates what two speakers say (one repeats the other, one is a beat behind, one is a number later, the next number), the words must show exactly that relation: a repeat is the same word, "one number later" is the following number, "the next number" continues the sequence. A count goes one way (up or down) by one each time across all clips unless the story says it restarts.
- **Plan checks** (added to the retry complaint in `planStory`, after `checkBreakdown`, which is unchanged so the Python port test still agrees): `planSpeechIssues` (a shot without a dialogue line whose subject or action describes speech) and `sequenceIssues`. These two kinds get one more retry (three planner calls at most); every other plan failure keeps E4.2's single retry.
- **`speechActs` / `speechProse`**: a sentence outside `<d>` that counts aloud, recites, chants, calls out, whispers, murmurs, mutters, repeats or echoes someone's number or word, reads aloud, or whose mouth, lips or voice "move in a steady count", with no `<d>` in the sentence, is flagged (the audit's own `SPEECH_WITHOUT_WORDS` covers say/speak/ask; this adds the verbs it lacks). Noun readings ("the whispers of the wind", "a phone call") are not flagged. Finding `speech_prose`; repaired by the existing targeted repair.
- **`sequenceIssues(lines, story)`**: a line is a sequence line when it is numbers with at most one label word (`parseNumbers`, `sequenceTokens`: digits, number words, "twenty-one", "one hundred and five"). `storyRelations(story)` reads phrase classes from the story: echo ("half a beat behind", "echoes", "repeats her", "in unison", "after her"), "one number later / ahead / earlier" (offset +1 / -1), "the next number", count down / backwards, restart / start over, and the numbers the story names (ordinals, digits, "to ten"). Checks: when the story relates two speakers, the film's merged count steps by exactly one (or repeats, if the story has an echo) in one direction; otherwise each speaker's own stream does; a jump or reset is accepted only when the story names the number or says restart; each relation the story states must occur (a repeated number, a pair one apart) and "the next number" must be the previous number plus one. Anything the story does not state and the lines break is an issue for the planner retry; stories with no relation words and no numbers are untouched (`unverified` lists a relation the lines cannot show).
- Final prompts get the same checks in `filmchecks.mjs` (`film_checks.json`: `speakerIds`, `sequence`, per clip `speechWithoutLine`).

## Fix 5: music-like words in the sound fields, and the score setting (`soundvocab.mjs`, `bible.mjs`, `writer.mjs`, `hybrid46`)
- `musicLikeHits(text, field)`: CORE words (hum, drone, resonance/resonant/resonate, chord, thrum, overtone, harmonic, reverberation, sustained or low/high note) are flagged in `overall_soundscape` and in the description outside `<d>`; SOUND words (tone, swell, pulse, throb, note) only in `overall_soundscape` where every word names a sound, and `tone` also in a description sentence that has no speaker marker (a voice described as a tone is allowed). Ordinary uses (humid, humble, her pulse, the sea swells, notes on a table) are not flagged.
- Finding `soundvocab` goes to the existing targeted repair ("name each sound by its concrete physical source and action"). Last resort in `film.mjs` after the repair rounds: `dropItems` removes the list item that still holds such a word from the soundscape (never when nothing would be left).
- The writer's facts replace the vendored rule's "use hum, drone" suggestion (the vendored `shot_prose.md` is unchanged): sounds are named by physical source; the music-like words are not used in the soundscape or the description.
- **Setting `--score off|on` (env `E4_SCORE`, default off).** off: `non_diegetic_music` is the sentinel `N/A` and the sweep above runs. on: the bible asks for one extra field, `score` (item 9 of the bible prompt, only when on): one instrumental paragraph in the guide's form (instrumentation, tempo, dynamics), chosen to suit the story's genre and mood, 25 to 70 words, no absence word, no vocal part (`scoreIssues`); every clip's `non_diegetic_music` is exactly that text (`hybrid46/finish.mjs`, the facts), the audit's sentinel finding is dropped (`hybrid46/validate.mjs`), and the sweep still keeps hum, drone and the like out of the soundscape and the description (the score belongs in the music field).
- **Bible prompt changes (exactly):** item 7 (voices) gains "Describe a voice by age, register, pace, accent and the physical place or device it carries from; use no word a listener hears as music (hum, drone, resonance, resonant, tone, chord, swell, pulse). A voice has no image, no acting master and no <Subject> number in the prompt: it is written as its voice description followed by its speaker id."; the CHECK list gains "without a music-like word (hum, drone, resonant, chord, swell, pulse)" in the voicePrompt rule; with `--score on` item 9 and a `score` entry in the reply shape and the check list. Nothing else of the bible prompt changed.

# E4.10 FROZEN

E4.10 is E4.9 (`planpath49/`, tag `e4.9-frozen`) plus the three fixes the real Swift run of E4.9 on the tears story asked for (memory `2026-10-09-e4.9-state-fixes.md`). `planpath49/` and older directories are untouched; E4.10 is the copy `planpath410/`. Verify: `node planpath410/verify-frozen.mjs` (exit 1 if any listed file changed); git tag `e4.10-frozen`. Model and transport as E4.7 to E4.9 (Swift 1.5 on `/ninfer-swift15`, `ninfer-messages`, decisions thinking off). Generic rules only (the DON'T-CHEAT firewall: no story word in any rule table; `antioverfit.test.mjs` polices it).

## What changed
1. **The base line never holds the opening wording of an axis whose later value the subject carries (`state.mjs`, `bible.mjs`).**
   - Example (real E4.9 strings, tears story, clip 2). Before: `<Subject 1> is Marisol, a young woman with dry cheeks, clear eyes, dry dark hair, a faded indigo cotton blouse, ..., now with wet tear trails down both cheeks, eyes red-rimmed; ...`. After: `<Subject 1> is Marisol, a young woman with dry dark hair, a faded indigo cotton blouse, ..., now with wet tear trails down both cheeks, eyes red-rimmed; ...`. The hair is kept because the hair axis is not carried in clip 2; in clip 3 (hair carried) it goes too.
   - **Prevention at the bible.** The `OPENING STATE ONLY` block of the bible prompt now also lists, per trace axis, the words of the part it changes (`leave_out`: cheeks, eyes) and tells the bible not to write a phrase about them, nor the opening value, in `appearsAs` (the base line). `bibleBaseWordingIssues` is the check (findings start `BASE WORDING:`): it sends the bible back, with a third attempt like the opening-state check, and after the last attempt it is only reported (`attempts[].issues`), never a failure of the bible, because the assembly guard below covers it.
   - **Deterministic guard at subject-line assembly.** `stripOpeningWording(description, items)`; `withCarriedState` applies it to the subject's base description for every axis the subject carries in this clip. The description is read as units split at commas, `and` and `with`; the first unit (the subject's own noun phrase) is never removed. A later unit is dropped when it is the opening value (or a part of it), or names a part of the axis (a word every option of the axis shares) that a camera sees on a body or garment (`VISIBLE_NOUNS`) in four content words or fewer; a unit that only holds a word of the opening value that no later option has ("dry", "unlit", "whole") loses that word. Idempotent; the clip before the change, and any subject that carries nothing, keep the whole base line (byte-identical to E4.9). Nothing else of the line is touched.
   - **Check.** `baseContradictions({carried, references, subjectDefinitions})` lists the units of a carried subject's base that still hold opening wording; `stateReport` reports it per clip and as `baseContradictions` (target 0).
2. **The name rule reads the phrase, not the tokens (`state.mjs nowWithIssue`).** E4.9 rejected any option holding the subject's own name or head noun anywhere, which rejected natural values ("all electric lights blazing", yard and rain states) and starved the planner (erotica carried 3 items against 7 before the rule). Now an option is rejected only for the redundant forms: it starts with the subject (after an article or possessive: "candle burning low", "Nandini's cheeks wet"), or the subject's noun is the head noun of its first noun group ("rumpled quilt", "oil-slicked boat", "wooden boat glistening with oil"). A part phrase that merely mentions the noun ("a thin scar under Tamsin's left eye", "soot clouding the lantern glass", "long cracks across the clay jar") passes, and every value of a generic or collective subject (an environment, a plural thing, lights, yard, rain, sky, crowd) passes the name rule. Added: a value that opens like a clause (and, when, while, as, that, a pronoun) does not read after "now with" and is rejected.
3. **The aftermath check asks for the right KIND of axis (`aftermath.mjs`).** A cue now needs a `visible_trace` axis of the character whose name or options hold a word of the cue's kind (`KIND_WORDS`): crying: tears, eyes, cheeks; sweating: sweat, skin; wet: wet, soaked, damp; torn: torn, ripped, seam; dirt: mud, dust, dirt; blood and wound; flushed; hair; smudged make-up. One sentence that cues several kinds for one person is satisfied by any of them. The issue names the kind and the axes the character has. The checklist in the planner prompt says to declare an axis of the matching kind.
4. **Tooling.** `freeze.mjs`, `verify-frozen.mjs`, `closure.mjs`, `replay-check.mjs`, `replay-state.mjs`, `trace-reads.mjs` point to `planpath410`. `test/e410.test.mjs` and `test/cases410.mjs` replace E4.9's; `antioverfit.test.mjs` allowlist gained the plain words of the new tables.

## Known weaknesses (not fixed here)
- The strip is lexical: it reads units split at commas, `and` and `with`, and removes a part only when the part word is a body or garment noun (`VISIBLE_NOUNS`); a prop's own part word ("glass", "wick") is not removed for being a part, only through the opening value or its distinctive words.
- A base line written as one long phrase without commas keeps its opening wording unless a distinctive opening word can be removed from it.
- A bible that still names the part after three attempts is only reported; the assembly guard then removes it from the line, but the reference picture was made from that bible.
